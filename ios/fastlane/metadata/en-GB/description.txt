Every game you've beaten, in one diary.

Beaten is a log for the games you play. Keep track of what you're playing now, everything you've finished, and the ones you've mastered, then look back on years of play.

LOG EVERY RUN
Mark a game as Playing, Beaten, Mastered or Shelved. Add the platform, when you started and finished (to the day, the month or just the year, if that's all you remember), and whether it was a replay.

RATE AND REVIEW
Give it up to five stars, in halves, and write down what you thought. Mark spoilers so nobody gets caught out.

BUILD SHELVES
All-timers, piles of shame, cosy gems: make lists of your own and add games while you log them.

SEARCH ANY GAME
Find games from every era and platform, with covers and artwork, and filter by console.

YOUR YEAR AT A GLANCE
See what you're playing now, what you've finished recently, and how many games you've beaten this year.

ONE ACCOUNT, PHONE AND WEB
The app works with your account on beaten.games, where your diary also lives as a public profile you can share. Sign in with Apple or with your email.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
No ads and no tracking. Download everything you've logged, or delete your account, from inside the app at any time.

Game data and artwork come from IGDB.
