1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Beaten is a video game diary, like Letterboxd for games. Players log the games they're playing, have beaten and have mastered, with dates, platform, a star rating and an optional review, and organise them into shelves. They can follow friends, see what they've been playing, and react to and comment on their reviews. Extras turn the diary into shareable things: a "Console Chronicles" timeline of the consoles they've owned, share cards and a "Year in review" video.
It's for adults and teens who play video games and want a record of what they've played and finished. It solves "what did I play last year, and what did I think of it?", and gives friends a place to compare notes. The app is free, with no ads, purchases or subscriptions.

2. HOW TO USE IT
- Sign in with the demo account in the Sign-In Information section (email and password), or with Sign in with Apple. New accounts can be made with "Create an account" (it opens beaten.games/register, then sign in in the app).
- Home: stats, now playing, recently beaten. Tap "Log a game", search (e.g. "Hades"), tap + then choose Beaten; add a rating and review and save.
- Library: your diary (filter with the top-right button) and your shelves.
- Activity: what people you follow logged. The demo account follows another account with reviews. Under a review: tap the smiley to react, tap Comment to comment. Long-press a review or comment for "Report", which emails our support team with it quoted. Tap the person's name > ... (top right) > Block or Report. Blocking ends follows both ways and hides each from the other's activity and comments.
- Bell (top right of Activity): notifications when people react to or comment on your reviews; the gear sets how often they're emailed.
- Profile: followers, Find people, Extras (Console Chronicles, Share cards, Year in review), Download my data, Sign out, Delete account.
- Account deletion: Profile > Delete account. Password accounts confirm with the password; Sign in with Apple accounts type their username or email. It deletes the account and all its data immediately.

Moderation: everyone agrees to the Terms of use (beaten.games/terms) at sign-up, which have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Reports go to support@beaten.games and are acted on within 24 hours: the content is removed and the account suspended or closed. Review authors can delete any comment on their reviews.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- IGDB (Twitch): game names, cover art and release data.
- Sign in with Apple: optional sign-in.
- Resend: transactional email (confirmation, password reset, notification emails).
- Amazon Web Services (Lightsail): hosts our API and database (beaten.games).
- Wikimedia Commons: console photos in Console Chronicles, under their CC licences (credited at beaten.games/credits).
On the website only (not in the app): optional Steam and RetroAchievements imports, using their public APIs.
No AI services, payment processors, ads or analytics SDKs.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region; the interface is in English.
